## A. 原创英文阅读｜English Reading

**1.** After a challenging exam, Lin Cheng and her study partner left the campus café. Rain blurred the lights between them, but neither wanted to end the conversation. He was curious about her plans and expected a quick goodbye.

> 考试结束后，林澄与同伴走出校园咖啡馆。雨模糊了路灯，他们却还不想结束交谈。

**2.** “I used to assume I had to face every challenge alone,” she said. “You encourage me to concentrate, even when I feel uncertain.” He tried to avoid making the moment too serious, yet his affection was obvious.

> 她坦白：“我曾以为所有困难都得独自承担。你总能在我不确定时鼓励我专注。”他想避免让气氛太严肃，但喜欢之情已经很明显。

**3.** They had learned to adapt to each other’s different schedules. The distance between their homes was inconvenient, but it did not determine their decision to meet again.

> 他们学会适应不同的日程；两人住得远，却没有影响再次见面的决定。

**4.** “If I had one more hour, I would spend it walking with you,” he said. She smiled. “Then let’s make a plan as soon as the rain stops.” What motivated them was not a reward for passing the exam; it was the chance to know each other better.

> “如果我还有一个小时，我想和你一起散步。”她微笑：“那雨停以后就约好吧。”学习之外，让他们心动的是了解彼此的机会。

## C. 语法拆解｜Grammar

非真实现在条件：If + 过去式，would + 动词原形。as soon as 后使用一般现在时表达将来的时间。 

## D. 理解与练习｜Active Recall

1. **选择：** If I ___ one more hour, I would spend it with you.  
   A. had / B. have / C. will have / D. am having
2. **阅读理解：** Why did they decide to meet again?
3. **应用：** 用 If I had..., I would... 写一句关于再次见面的原创英文句子。

<details><summary>展开参考答案</summary>

1. **A. had**。与现在事实相反的假设：If + 过去式，主句 would + 动词原形。
2. They enjoyed knowing and encouraging each other, not just studying for rewards.
3. 开放题：语法与意义正确即可，不限制唯一表达。

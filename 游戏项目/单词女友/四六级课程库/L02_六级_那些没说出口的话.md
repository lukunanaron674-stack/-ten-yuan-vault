## A. 原创英文阅读｜English Reading

**1.** Two weeks later, their study sessions had developed a subtle rhythm, but the ambiguity of their growing closeness was difficult to ignore. Their mutual trust was still new.

> 两周后，他们的自习渐渐有了一种微妙的固定节奏，但关系变近的含义依旧暧昧。信任刚刚形成。

**2.** Lin Cheng could interpret a complex passage immediately, yet she could not always convey what she felt. When he arrived late, she felt unexpectedly vulnerable, caught in a contradiction between pride and affection.

> 林澄能迅速解释难句，却不容易传达自己的感受。对方迟到时，她突然感到脆弱，陷入自尊与喜欢之间的矛盾。

**3.** “I should have told you I was delayed,” he said. He apologized without trying to dismiss her concern. His direct words reassured her, although they could not erase the missed hour.

> “我应该提前告诉你我会迟到。”他认真道歉，并未轻视她的担忧。直白的解释让她安心，却不能抹掉失约的一小时。

**4.** She recognized that empathy, not perfection, was the basis of an intimate relationship. Their compromise was to send a message when plans changed. As they anticipated the next exam, they learned to be honest even when honesty felt uncomfortable.

> 她明白共情比完美更重要。两人达成约定：计划有变就及时沟通。备考期间，他们逐渐学会在不自在时也坦诚。

## C. 语法拆解｜Grammar

although + 完整让步从句；should have + 过去分词 表示过去本应做而未做；结合语境推断说话人的真实意图。

## D. 理解与练习｜Active Recall

1. **选择：** He apologized, ___ the mistake could not be undone.  
   A. although / B. because of / C. despite of / D. until that
2. **阅读理解：** What did the two students agree to change?
3. **应用：** What made Lin Cheng feel vulnerable? 用一整句英文说明。

<details><summary>展开参考答案</summary>

1. **A. although**。although 引导让步状语从句，后面接完整句子。
2. They agreed to communicate promptly when plans changed.
3. 开放题：语法与意义正确即可，不限制唯一表达。

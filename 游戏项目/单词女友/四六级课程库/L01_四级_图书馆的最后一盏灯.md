## A. 原创英文阅读｜English Reading

**1.** At 10:45 p.m., the library was almost empty. Lin Cheng, a twenty-two-year-old graduate student, was trying to maintain her focus when someone approached her table.

> 22:45，图书馆几乎空了。22岁的研究生林澄正努力集中注意力，一名同样备考的学生走向她的桌边。

**2.** “Could I sit here?” the visitor asked. She hesitated, then nodded. His awkward smile made an unexpectedly warm impression.

> “我可以坐这儿吗？”她犹豫片刻，点了点头。对方略显局促的笑容给她留下温暖的第一印象。

**3.** They discovered that they were preparing for the same English exam. As they compared notes, he made a quiet remark about her careful handwriting. She tried to respond confidently, but her face grew warm.

> 他们发现彼此都在准备英语考试。对照笔记时，他轻声称赞她字写得认真。她想镇定地回应，却还是红了脸。

**4.** When the lights began to dim, he said, “I appreciate your help. Would you like to study together again?” There was no hidden pressure in his intention—only the potential for a new encounter.

> 灯光渐暗，他说：“谢谢你帮我。下次还愿意一起学习吗？”没有催促，只有一次新相遇的可能。

## C. 语法拆解｜Grammar

过去进行时 = was/were + doing；Could I…? 表示委婉请求。注意过去动作与现在动作的差异。

## D. 理解与练习｜Active Recall

1. **选择：** At 10:45 p.m., Lin Cheng ___ to maintain her focus.  
   A. was trying / B. tries / C. has try / D. will trying
2. **阅读理解：** What did the visitor ask for at the end?
3. **应用：** 把‘我们正在整理笔记时，图书馆熄灯了’翻译成英文。

<details><summary>展开参考答案</summary>

1. **A. was trying**。明确过去某一时刻正在持续的动作，用过去进行时 was trying。
2. They agreed to study together again.
3. 开放题：语法与意义正确即可，不限制唯一表达。

## 可用图片/素材页

- [Wikimedia Commons: Cyberpunk city with not enough funds...](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cyberpunk_city_with_not_enough_funds_to_protect_against_the_rising_sea,_using_the_polluted_water_for_cooler_air.jpg)  
  CC0，AI 生成图。适合表达“赛博朋克不只是霓虹灯，也是污染、贫困、气候焦虑和城市失修”。

- [Wikimedia Commons: Algorithmically-generated AI-generated artwork of a cyberpunk cityscape](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Algorithmically-generated_AI-generated_artwork_of_a_cyberpunk_cityscape.png)  
  AI 生成城市景观，适合做宽幅城市氛围参考。

- [Pexels: Neon city street at night](https://www.pexels.com/photo/neon-city-street-at-night-4132321/)  
  现实城市夜景，适合提取“湿地面、冷光、街灯、城市孤独感”。

- [Pexels: City Street at Night with Neon Signs](https://www.pexels.com/photo/city-street-at-night-with-neon-signs-36676322/)  
  雨夜、霓虹招牌、车流和街道反光，可作为传统赛博朋克街景参考。

- [Unsplash: Cyberpunk City Photos](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/cyberpunk-city)  
  免费图片集合，适合继续挑选城市、霓虹、未来街区素材。

## 采集提示

- 不只收“好看的霓虹”，也收破旧、拥挤、污染、监控、广告过载的画面。
- 每张图旁边记录：作者、来源链接、许可证、可用场景。

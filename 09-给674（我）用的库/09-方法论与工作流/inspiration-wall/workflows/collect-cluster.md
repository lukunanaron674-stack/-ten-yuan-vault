# Collect → Cluster → Judge

1. Convert the seed into 3–5 structural search clusters.
2. Search 5–6 references per cluster.
3. Store source metadata.
4. Score candidates.
5. Judge the top candidates separately.
6. Keep 2–3 strong references per cluster.
7. Extract transferable structure from accepted references.
8. Do not keep weak references just to hit a quota.

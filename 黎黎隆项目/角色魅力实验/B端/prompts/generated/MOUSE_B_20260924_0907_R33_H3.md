### Fixed H3 prompt

```text
2D hand-drawn cinematic single-character sequence, vertical 9:16, 15 seconds.

STYLE IDENTITY LOCK: preserve the verified drawing language, proportions, materials and original colors of Picture 1 and Picture 2. Keep the exact mouse-eared mutant girl's short white hair with eye-covering fringe and small upward curl, large translucent pale-violet mouse-ear structures, small slender body, oversized pale robe, dark collar, cuffs and hem blocks, diagonal strap and side satchel. Preserve the forest-lake identity and its warm-sky/cool-water color relationship. Reference fidelity overrides generic style wording.

VISUAL EXPRESSION VARIABLE: use restrained readable 2D motion, compatible variable-weight hand-drawn lines, thin watercolor-like shading only where consistent with the references, soft diffuse light, muted shadows and quiet negative space. For this controlled version, clarity comes from a fixed observational frame rather than camera movement or montage.

CHARACTER (Picture 1): show exactly one physical instance of the established mouse-eared mutant girl. If Picture 1 contains multiple identity views, they all describe this one character and must never become multiple people. Preserve her cautious behavior, clothing and accessories.

ENVIRONMENT (Picture 2): preserve the same verified lake, layered forest, spatial continuity and established palette logic of SCENE-02. Picture 2 defines the place, not a mandatory crop. Do not invent a bridge, dock, stones, roots, buildings or props. The water image is only the optical reflection of the same girl, physically dependent on her position and deformed only by the water surface.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION: use one fixed medium-wide three-quarter side view for the entire segment. Keep her full route, feet, nearby water and dependent reflection readable together in one frame. Place her on the shore-side third, retain negative space over the lake, preserve one screen axis, and do not push, pan, track, zoom or cut.

ACTION: the girl walks slowly parallel to the shore at a safe distance from the water. Her dependent reflection remains causally aligned beneath and beside her. One small natural ripple crosses the reflected outline and briefly deforms its shape without giving it independent motion. She notices the mismatch, stops, and takes one short half-step back from the water. Hold the entire cause and response in the same fixed frame. Her near mouse-ear structure turns toward the ripple first while her torso, hands and feet stay still; her eyes follow a moment later. End on a clear held pose: she remains present and observant without approaching the water.

AUDIO DESIGN: quiet lake air, faint leaves, soft water movement, robe and strap friction, careful steps and one localized ripple. A thin non-melodic low breath tone tightens at the reflected distortion, falls nearly silent on the half-step back and remains suspended at the cut. Audio clarifies cause, attention order and caution; it does not impose song structure or MV rhythm.

NEGATIVE: no second physical character, duplicate girl, clone, twin, separate water person, autonomous reflection, emerging reflection, independent shadow double, split screen or four-person character sheet; no rabbit ears, ordinary replacement ears, tail, horns, wings, armor, weapon, fitted battle costume or adult muscular body; no complete reflection moving on its own or staying delayed after the ripple; no camera push, pan, tracking, zoom, cutaway, close-up or axis change; no bridge, dock, stones, roots, buildings or props; no splash, fall, run, panic, dance, lip-sync, montage or music-video cutting; no cyan-white energy humanoid, glossy 3D render, hard synthetic digital edges, text, subtitle, logo or watermark.
```

## 镜1 · 废墟苏醒（6秒）

| 参数 | 值 |
|---|---|
| 十元 | xz 临界状态 |
| 镜头 | 手持微晃 → 缓慢稳定 |
| 色调 | 冷蓝 + 低饱和 |
| 光源 | 单一高窗裂缝光 |
| 构图 | 人物在画面下1/3，上方大量留黑 |

**LTX-Video prompt（本地快速迭代）：**
```text
A solitary cloaked figure slowly sits up on a stone floor inside a vast ruined hall, cold blue moonlight shaft from a high crack in the ceiling, dust particles floating in the light beam, handheld slight camera shake then slowly stabilizes, low saturation, dark fantasy atmosphere, rough stone textures, deep shadows filling 70 percent of frame, cinematic 24fps
negative: warm colors, crowd, daylight, clean architecture, symmetrical
```

**Wan2.2 prompt（云端精修）：**
```text
Cinematic dark fantasy shot: a lone cloaked figure rises from lying on broken stone tiles in an enormous ruined gothic hall, single shaft of cold blue-white light piercing through a high jagged crack in the domed ceiling 30 meters above, dust motes dancing slowly in the light column, the figure is small in frame occupying bottom third, camera starts with subtle handheld sway then gradually stabilizes as if the character is anchoring themselves, thick impenetrable shadows in all corners, rough hewn stone walls with patches of dark moss, no other light sources, film grain subtle, 16fps with slight frame judder for handmade feel, 6 seconds
negative: warm amber light, torchlight, daylight, bright sky visible, clean polished stone, symmetrical composition, sharp digital look
```

**十元到视觉的翻译：**
- xz 的"漂流感" → 手持晃动 → 正在稳定（锚定过程）
- xz 的"临界" → 光与暗的边界刚好切过人物
- z（辅助）→ 光束 = 远方的光点/执念

## 镜2 · 巨塔之下（8秒）

| 参数 | 值 |
|---|---|
| 十元 | n 克 xz（承载反制漂流） |
| 镜头 | 缓慢仰拍 + 水平摇镜 |
| 色调 | 暖琥珀光从下方渗入冷蓝 |
| 光源 | 塔底门缝暖光 + 天空冷月光 双光源竞争 |
| 构图 | 塔占画面左2/3，人物在右下角仰望 |

**LTX-Video prompt：**
```text
A small cloaked figure stands at the base of a colossal dark stone tower looking up, the tower fills most of the frame, warm amber light seeps from a narrow door crack at the tower base casting long soft shadows, cold moon remains in the sky above, the two light temperatures compete creating an unsettling warmth, slow tilt-up camera movement revealing the tower impossible height, low poly style stone blocks with AI generated moss and rust texture, dark fantasy atmosphere
negative: full daylight, multiple towers, crowd, clean modern architecture, symmetrical
```

**Wan2.2 prompt：**
```text
Epic low-angle shot: a tiny hooded figure at the bottom right of frame gazing up at a monolithic dark stone tower that stretches beyond the top of frame, the tower surface is rough cyclopean masonry with patches of dark green moss and rust-red iron streaks, a thin line of warm amber light escapes from a barely-open iron door at the tower base illuminating the figure feet and casting long soft shadows across the frost-covered ground, cold blue moonlight still visible in the upper sky creating a two-temperature lighting conflict, the tower seems impossibly tall suggesting it pierces the cloud layer, the figure stance shifts from lost to curious, slow majestic tilt-up, dark fantasy low-poly aesthetic with AI-generated pbr textures, 8 seconds
negative: bright sunlight, multiple buildings, modern architecture, clean surfaces, cheerful atmosphere, symmetrical framing
```

**十元到视觉：**
- n 克 xz：塔 = 承载/容器，它的存在让漂流者停下
- 双光源竞争：暖（n的安全）vs 冷（xz的漂流记忆）
- 仰拍 + 塔的巨大 = n 的压倒性稳定

```text
Wide static shot of a snowy wasteland at night, the dark tower on the far left edge of frame with its door now closed and the warm light inside fading to nothing, a tiny cloaked figure walks away across the snow to the right, leaving a single trail of footprints, cold blue moonlight dominates but there is a subtle residual warmth in the color grade as if the memory of shelter lingers, the figure becomes smaller and smaller, dark fantasy atmosphere, melancholic but not tragic
```

**Wan2.2 prompt：**
```text
Wide master shot: a vast snow-covered wasteland under cold blue night sky, the monolithic dark stone tower stands at the far left edge of frame now with its iron door firmly shut, the thin amber light from the door crack fading to black as if the tower is going back to sleep, a tiny cloaked figure walks away toward the right side of frame leaving a single trail of dark footprints in the pristine snow, the color grade is predominantly cold blue moonlight BUT with a barely perceptible residual warmth in the midtones as if the memory of shelter has stained the image itself, the figure shrinks to less than 2 percent of frame height by shot end, the tower remains silent and immense, this is not tragedy but a completed cycle, dark fantasy low-poly aesthetic, camera locked static, 7 seconds
```

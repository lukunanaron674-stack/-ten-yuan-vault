## 01｜锁定原则与执行前门禁
- **画幅**：16:9 横屏，二维手绘平涂、约4–5头身微Q比例、干净墨线与局部柔和暖冷光；以Claire/Lucas/Marcel实际审核角色原图与对应巴黎母场景为最高身份/画风依据。禁止把旧黎黎隆机械龙/青白光/主教服装等误写进本片。
- **Picture1** 代表当前镜头实际已审核人物/动作或多人合成首帧，**Picture2** 代表当前镜头的真实场景/空间参考；如多角色或细节镜缺乏验证合成图，标记 BLOCKED_MISSING_REFERENCE，不可直接宣称已具备。多视图角色卡只代表**一人**。
- **本包的每镜英文描述词并非彼此连续的一条H3视频命令**。渲染时拼接 [GLOBAL STYLE+REFERENCE LOCK] + [本镜英文SHOT] + [NEGATIVE LOCK]，各镜独立单镜生成，剪取目标时长；不要命令H3在一段内硬切5–7次。
- **建议有真实稳定性证据的约5.17秒短镜源素材，剪辑成1.3–4.3秒目标长度**；若真实工作流提供其他合法帧数，按实测配置运行。镜头目标时长不是可直接填入H3的任意精确生成时长。正式影片保留卡1=14.5s、卡2-1=12s、卡2-2=13s、卡3=20s，合计59.5s。
- 卡3原剧情20s以**10s＋10s剪辑块**交付，不是一个超过单次上限的H3生成。卡1的14.5s沿用原审核候选（15s硬上限内），不误套EP2偏好14.3s而擅改故事。
- **真人/物件连续性**：Claire同一服装和手机，卡1的设计布料、书店的一本旧书与单一信封、Lucas与钢琴、书店门和Marcel位置必须跨镜一致。严格前后景方位与视线轴；不同地点用剪辑明确省略时间。
- **关键H待审**：奇幻景象具体形式；信封是否能从书店带走；街头琴声真实来源；卡3地铁钢琴地点；多角色同框首帧/地铁母场景。相关镜头先做文字/图候选，未批不放行生成。
- **参考图接线故障经验**：不得仅在prompt里提Picture就算绑定；A端实测 ref_images 或对应工作流图片节点是否真正起作用，记录输入图SHA、workflow、seed(真实时才填写)、run_id、输出MP4与首中尾帧。纯文字跑出新脸视为失败。
- **H06失败经验**：禁止把一镜的有效叙事缩水成仅嘴/手微动。必须看到动作首态、整体身体/道具受力、结果；群像只有在原图里存在才可出现。

### GLOBAL STYLE + REFERENCE LOCK（每条H3镜头前拼接）
```text
Cinematic 2D hand-drawn animation, horizontal 16:9, clean expressive ink linework, matte flat cel colors and gentle restrained two-tone warm/cool shading, European Paris everyday-life atmosphere, small stylized 4–5-head-tall character proportions matching the approved character sheet exactly. SOURCE PRIORITY: the actual approved image for this shot is the sole visual authority for character face, hairstyle, costume, body scale, visible props and viewpoint; the approved environment plate defines existing room or street geometry, materials and palette. Preserve identity and screen direction across neighboring shots. A real single continuous camera take inside this one shot only; do not simulate a multi-shot montage. Use exactly the established onscreen people required in this storyboard shot, not duplicates or additional invented main characters. Film acting must be observable: start pose, trigger, complete physical action, final result. Faces and hands remain consistent with the reference. Dialogue and precise sounds are post-production cues, not claims of native H3 audio.
```

### NEGATIVE LOCK（每条H3镜头末尾拼接）
```text
NEGATIVE: no 3D CGI or glossy plastic materials, photorealism, painterly identity drift, changing outfit, long realistic body proportions, extra limbs or fingers, extra copies of Claire/Lucas/Marcel, duplicated envelope, prop morphing, architecture relocation, illegible or invented text, unplanned camera orbit, impossible jump in action or scale, auto-cut montage, split-screen or four-panel character sheet, subtitles, logo or watermark. No extra spoken dialogue, exaggerated crying, meaningless idle mouth loop, magical glow without separately approved VFX. Keep only the number of background extras established by the approved shot reference.
```
